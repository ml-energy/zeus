## PyTorch Support

As a project part of the PyTorch Ecosystem criteria, the maintainers are committed to supporting at least the latest two minor releases of PyTorch, and will continue to do so for at least the next 12 months.
However, Zeus only depends on very stable PyTorch APIs (e.g., `torch.cuda.synchronize()`), so it is expected that Zeus will work with nearly any reasonably recent version of PyTorch.
